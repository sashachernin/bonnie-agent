## The idea

Gift buyers often know the memories that would delight a parent, grandparent, partner, or retiring colleague, but not how to turn them into a polished present. Sell a self-service printable book of crosswords, word searches, letter scrambles, and a final hidden message built from those details. The buyer reviews every answer and clue before paying a proposed $14 for the PDF. An optional print partner can come later; version one earns from the download.

## A customer example

Hypothetically, Nina searches Etsy for a personalized puzzle book for her father's seventieth birthday. She follows a listing to the builder, enters twelve short memories such as a nickname, first car, favorite lake, and family saying, then corrects one generated clue. She previews a 12-page large-print book, pays $14, and downloads both the gift PDF and answer key in about ten minutes. Her father receives an activity whose answers are his own stories, rather than an ordinary puzzle book with only his name on its cover.

## Who pays, and for what

The initial buyer is an English-speaking person with a gift deadline and a recipient who enjoys word puzzles. The same first version serves birthdays, anniversaries, retirements, reunions, and family holidays; it does not serve children who need illustrated activities or expert-level crossword solvers.

Demand is evidenced by purchases, not merely complaints. Etsy's current personalized-crossword-book results show 196-plus items, including a $29.42 cover-personalized book with 206 reviews and a $28.50 personalized word-search book with 188 reviews ([market results](https://www.etsy.com/market/personalized_crossword_puzzle_book)). A separate custom-crossword result has 11,000-plus reviews, although weddings dominate that adjacent demand. These counts support an active gift category, not demand for this exact multi-puzzle format.

## What the AI agent would build

Version one needs an occasion picker, a structured memory form, clue editor, instant page preview, Stripe checkout, and PDF plus answer-key download. Deterministic puzzle generators place words, verify every solution, prevent accidental duplicate answers, and render large-print layouts. A low-cost language model may suggest plain clues from buyer-supplied facts, but the buyer must approve them; failure falls back to a literal clue template.

The coding agent builds the generators, print layout, validation tests, temporary storage, payment flow, and deletion after seven days. The hardest risk is producing varied, solvable grids from short or awkward answers. Version one excludes open-ended biography writing, copyrighted characters, physical fulfilment, accounts, and manual puzzle composition.

## Launch and ongoing maintenance

The owner arranges hosting, Stripe, an Etsy shop, privacy terms, and finished sample books. Etsy allows personalized made-to-order inputs and original seller-designed digital downloads, but requires accurate images and disclosure when artificial intelligence is used ([personalization rules](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344528-How-to-Offer-Personalized-Listings), [creativity rules](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/)).

For the first ten buyers, list three real examples aimed at concrete searches: personalized puzzle book for grandpa, retirement memory puzzle book, and anniversary story puzzle book. Etsy already surfaces reviewed products for the broader query and explicitly lets sellers accept custom digital files; ranking is unverified. The fallback is permission-based demonstrations in gift-planning communities, whose promotion rules must be checked first. Estimate 6–10 hours monthly checking output and support, plus 12–20 hours making seasonal samples and improving listings; code handles each order without bespoke fulfilment.

## Why now

No platform change created the need. September is a useful testing window because Etsy's holiday merchandising guidance tells sellers to prepare early and identifies personalization, games, keepsakes, parents, and grandparents as holiday gift routes ([guide](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/22817661229)). That supports timing, not sales. The durable entry is marketplace demand split across recipient and occasion searches, while an instant complete preview can make an unfamiliar seller easier to trust.

## What exists today

[Chart the Magic](https://chartthemagic.com/puzzle-books) offers a memories-based mixed-puzzle PDF for $9.99 and is the strongest direct substitute. [Enigma Cabinet](https://www.enigmacabinet.com/) starts at 109 kr on the page served during research for a printed book that hides one familiar name. [Thoughtful Puzzles](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1410546239/custom-crossword-puzzle-book) charges $28.98 for 200 printed crosswords with a personalized cover and photo. Etsy also shows single personalized digital puzzles around $2.59–$11.81 and a personalized digital book at $19.99.

Chart the Magic is cheaper and already offers deep personalization. This entrant cannot claim invention. Its reason to be chosen is distribution and occasion merchandising: useful finished examples appear where Etsy shoppers already search, while the product previews a whole story-based book rather than asking them to commission a seller or accept cover-only personalization. Whether that earns marketplace attention is the central unverified premise.

## How it makes money

The proposal is $14 per PDF. Seventy-two sales produce $1,008 monthly revenue. At an assumed conservative 1% purchase rate, that needs 7,200 qualified listing visits; at an optimistic 4%, 1,800. Those are sensitivity cases, not forecasts, and repeat purchase should be rare.

Etsy charges $0.20 to list or renew a sold quantity and 6.5% of the order, with country-specific payment processing and possible 12%–15% offsite-ad fees ([official fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy)). Assume total marketplace and payment deductions of 10%–25%, plus under $30 monthly hosting and model costs: $1,008 revenue might leave roughly $726–$877 before tax and owner time. At 72 orders, budget three support hours, 6–10 maintenance hours, and 12–20 acquisition hours monthly. New recipient and occasion listings can continue acquisition, but 1,800 qualified visits is substantial for a new shop.

## The riskiest assumption

The killing belief is that buyers value a whole memory-based book enough to choose it over a $9.99 direct competitor, cheap Etsy templates, or a $29 printed book. In one week, show a complete watermarked sample to 30 qualified people who need a gift within 60 days for a puzzle-loving adult; recruit through permitted gift communities or direct personal invitations, not paid ads. Let each build a real preview. Pass if 15 finish, six download the watermarked proof, and four give a written $14 purchase commitment; fail if 15 finish and none commits. Fewer than 15 completions is an inconclusive channel test. Ask committed buyers after the occasion whether the recipient solved it; repeat gifting remains unresolved.

## What I rejected

- A browser scavenger-hunt host failed standalone value because Roadblocked is fully free and several no-download competitors already charge $1.99–$25.
- A portable-power-station matcher failed the business case because multiple current free calculators already handle appliances, surge, losses, and product recommendations without a supported acquisition advantage.

## The part I would argue against

A sceptic would say this is a crowded, seasonal gift with a superior direct competitor at $9.99 and thousands of cheap Etsy puzzle templates. They would be right that code is easy to copy and Etsy visibility is hard. The case still merits one test because reviewed marketplace listings demonstrate purchases, the files fulfil automatically, and recipient-specific listings provide a concrete route to shoppers without persuading them to adopt software. Abandon if 15 qualified builders produce no $14 commitment, or if they consistently prefer Chart the Magic after seeing both.

The prior benchmark is **Keep Your Portfolio Without the Builder Bill** (`2026-09-22-morning-own-portfolio-export.md`). Test that benchmark first: its official export limitation, $39 price, and urgent cancellation intent make the benefit sharper. Move this puzzle idea ahead only if four qualified gift buyers commit at $14 while the portfolio test cannot reach ten owners or produces no commitments.
